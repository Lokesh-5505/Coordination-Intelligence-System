# Coordination Intelligence System — Mixed-Use Construction Build

A role-based web app that connects stakeholders, responsibilities, activities, changes, dependencies, approvals and actions for one realistic mixed-use construction project — with AI that explains who is affected when something changes.

## Sample project

"Riverside Mixed-Use Development" — retail podium, two residential towers, basement parking. Pre-loaded with a full cast and live state so the demo is convincing from the first screen:

- Client / Developer representative
- Project Owner and Project Manager (PMC)
- Lead Architect, Interior Designer
- Structural, MEP and Facade consultants
- Main Contractor, plus HVAC, electrical, glazing and joinery vendors
- Site Engineer and QA/Safety specialist

Pre-loaded content: ~40 tasks and deliverables with owners and due dates, dependency links between them, pending approvals, open issues, a change log, and one headline change event (a slab-level revision on Tower B) whose ripple effect the demo traces end to end.

## Sign-in and roles

Each person signs in with email and password. What they see is scoped to their role:

- Everyone sees the project overview, activity feed and project memory.
- "My work" shows only the tasks, approvals and actions assigned to that person.
- Approvers (client, PM, consultants) get an approvals queue where they can approve, reject or request changes.
- The PM/owner role can log changes, create actions, reassign owners and see the full stakeholder matrix.

Demo accounts for each role are seeded, and the sign-in page lists them so judges can switch personas instantly.

## Screens

1. **Overview dashboard** — health of the project: blocked items, overdue actions, pending approvals, unacknowledged alerts, and today's AI briefing.
2. **Stakeholders** — directory plus a responsibility matrix (who owns which activities and deliverables) and relationships between parties.
3. **Activities & tasks** — list and board views with owner, status, due date, dependencies, blocked reason.
4. **Dependencies** — visual map of what depends on what, highlighting the chain a change touches.
5. **Changes** — log a change or decision; each change opens an impact report.
6. **Approvals** — queue with status, requester, decision history.
7. **Actions** — assigned actions with status, due dates, and the change or issue that created them.
8. **Alerts** — in-app alert centre with badges; alerts are raised for impacts, blockers, delays and approval requests, and can be acknowledged.
9. **Project memory** — chronological, searchable history of changes, decisions, approvals and resolutions with who and when.
10. **Ask the project** — chat that answers questions over the project's real data.

## The coordination intelligence

Four AI capabilities, all grounded in the project's actual records:

- **Impact analysis** — describe a change in plain words; the system walks the dependency and responsibility graph and the AI returns affected stakeholders, tasks, approvals and deliverables, with severity and reasoning, plus estimated schedule knock-on.
- **Auto-drafted actions, approvals and alerts** — from that impact report the AI proposes the actions to create (with suggested owner and due date), the approvals required, and who to notify. The PM reviews and accepts with one tap; accepted items become real records and fire alerts.
- **Ask the project anything** — "what's blocking glazing?", "who owns the MEP shop drawings?", "what needs my approval?" — answered from live data with links to the underlying items.
- **Daily coordination briefing** — per-stakeholder summary of risks, blockers and next actions, regenerated on demand.

Deterministic graph traversal finds the candidate impacts; the AI ranks, explains and drafts. So the demo never shows an empty or invented result if AI is unavailable — the traversal result still renders.

## Responsive behaviour

Built mobile-first, since site teams are on phones: bottom tab navigation and stacked cards on mobile, sidebar with multi-column dashboard and side-by-side impact panels on desktop. The dependency map pans and zooms on touch and switches to a compact list on small screens.

## Technical notes

- Lovable Cloud for database, email/password auth and server logic; row-level security scopes every read and write to the signed-in user's project membership and role, with role checks in a separate roles table.
- Schema: `projects`, `stakeholders`, `roles`/`user_roles`, `responsibilities`, `activities` (tasks/deliverables), `dependencies` (edges with type), `changes`, `impacts`, `actions`, `approvals`, `alerts`, `memory_entries`.
- Full demo dataset seeded by database migration with literal inserts, including the demo accounts, so the first screen is populated.
- AI runs server-side through Lovable AI; impact analysis and drafting return structured results, chat and briefings stream. Graph traversal runs in the database/server layer and is passed to the model as grounded context.
- Alerts are in-app only: rows in `alerts` with realtime updates and unread badges. No email setup needed.

## Build order

1. Enable Cloud, schema + security + seed data, demo accounts.
2. Auth, role-scoped shell, navigation, overview dashboard.
3. Stakeholders, responsibilities, activities, dependencies with the graph map.
4. Changes, deterministic impact traversal, approvals, actions, alerts, project memory.
5. AI layer: impact reasoning, action/approval/alert drafting, ask-the-project chat, daily briefing.
6. Responsive polish and an end-to-end walkthrough of the Tower B slab change across three personas.
