Kill the coordination black hole nightmare
Too many people. Too many handoffs. Too little visibility.

Background
Architecture, interior design and construction projects involve multiple stakeholders including clients, architects, designers, project owners, project managers, consultants, contractors, vendors, site teams and specialists. Each stakeholder is responsible for different activities, decisions and deliverables, while project information is often distributed across WhatsApp, emails, calls, meetings, drawings, documents and spreadsheets.

As projects become more complex, this creates a coordination black hole where information may exist, but the relationship between that information and the people responsible for acting on it is unclear.

Problem
A change made by one stakeholder can affect multiple people, tasks, approvals and downstream activities. Identifying these impacts is often dependent on manual coordination and individual knowledge.

There is a need for a coordination intelligence layer that can understand:

Who is responsible for what
Who is affected by a change
What depends on what
What requires approval
What is blocked
What needs to happen next
Expected solution
Participants should develop a Coordination Intelligence System capable of connecting stakeholders, responsibilities, activities, changes, dependencies, approvals and actions.

Stakeholder & Role Management
Represent stakeholders, roles, responsibilities and relationships.

Project Activity & Change Tracking
Capture project activities, decisions, issues and changes.

Impact Identification
Identify stakeholders, tasks or activities potentially affected by a change.

Dependency Management
Represent dependencies between tasks, activities, approvals and deliverables.

Action Tracking
Create, assign and track required actions.

Approval Management
Identify and track required approvals.

Coordination Alerts
Notify or warn relevant stakeholders about changes, delays or dependencies.

Project Memory
Maintain a history of important changes, decisions, actions and resolutions.

Key requirements
A realistic project scenario
Multiple stakeholders
Stakeholder roles and responsibilities
Tasks / actions
A project change or event
Dependencies or relationships
Identification of affected stakeholders or actions
Status tracking
At least one coordination-intelligence capability
A working demonstration
integrate AI/ML and make it more responsive for mobile and desktop devices also.
